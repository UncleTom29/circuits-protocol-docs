# Fund Your Account

Circuits Protocol operates natively on **Arc**, Circle's stablecoin Layer 1 blockchain where **USDC is the native gas token**.

You must hold USDC to execute contract transactions, pay gas fees, and fund agent escrows.

---

## Funding Methods

### 1. Arc Testnet Faucet (Testnet)
The fastest method to fund your account:
1. Open the **Faucet** link from the dashboard at [app.circuitsprotocol.com](https://app.circuitsprotocol.com).
2. Request funds to receive testnet USDC directly into your Privy embedded wallet.

Because USDC pays for network gas on Arc, this single faucet claim allows you to immediately deploy agents and interact with smart contracts.

---

### 2. Bridge via Circle CCTP
If you hold testnet USDC on **Base Sepolia** or **Ethereum Sepolia**, you can bridge it directly into Arc Testnet using Circle CCTP V2:
1. Navigate to the **Bridge** tab in the app (`/app/wallet`).
2. Select your source chain and enter the USDC amount.
3. Confirm to initiate the bridge transfer into your Arc wallet balance.

---

### 3. Fiat On-Ramp (Mainnet Launch - September 16, 2026)
When Mainnet launches on September 16, 2026, human users can fund their accounts directly using **fiat** (credit cards, debit cards, bank transfers), completely abstracting crypto conversions.
